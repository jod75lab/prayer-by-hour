About Prayer by Hour

Prayer by Hour offers a quiet intention for each hour of the day, inviting us to pause and pray for one another.

The intentions follow your local time and the seasons of the year and the Church. Sundays have their own prayers. You can also choose to see estimated local sunrise and sunset times.

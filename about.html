About Prayer by Hour

Prayer by Hour offers a quiet intention for each hour of the day, inviting us to pause and pray for one another.

Each day of the week has its own prayers, from the night watch to the close of day. Come back at the same hour next week and you’ll find a familiar intention. Some prayers change with the season.

Choose General to follow the seasons where you live. The Catholic calendar shares those prayers in Ordinary Time. During Advent, Christmas, Lent, Holy Week, and Easter, one prayer in every four hours follows the season of the Church. This browser remembers your calendar choice in a cookie.

At noon on Catholic Sundays, the intention follows the Gospel for that day and includes its Scripture reference. The readings follow the General Roman Calendar and its three-year Sunday cycle.

The day follows your local clock, from midnight to midnight. You can choose your hemisphere and see estimated local sunrise and sunset times.
